QUESTION 1: A film club rents a camera for Rs. 1,200 per day and a microphone for Rs. 350 per day for 2 days. It receives a 10% discount on the total. What does it pay?

--- DIRECT PROMPT ---
 Rs. 2,790.

--- CHAIN-OF-THOUGHT ---
 **Step 1:** Calculate the cost of the camera for 2 days.  
\(1200 \text{ Rs/day} \times 2 \text{ days} = 2400 \text{ Rs}\)

**Step 2:** Calculate the cost of the microphone for 2 days.  
\(350 \text{ Rs/day} \times 2 \text{ days} = 700 \text{ Rs}\)

**Step 3:** Find the total cost before discount.  
\(2400 \text{ Rs} + 700 \text{ Rs} = 3100 \text{ Rs}\)

**Step 4:** Compute the 10% discount.  
\(3100 \text{ Rs} \times 0.10 = 310 \text{ Rs}\)

**Step 5:** Subtract the discount from the total cost.  
\(3100 \text{ Rs} - 310 \text{ Rs} = 2790 \text{ Rs}\)

**Final Answer:** 2790

QUESTION 2: A media lab has 12 editing computers. Each is used by 3 students in the morning and 2 in the afternoon. How many studentsessions occur in one day?

--- DIRECT PROMPT ---
 60 student sessions occur in one day.

--- CHAIN-OF-THOUGHT ---
 **Step 1:**  
Determine how many sessions occur on one computer in a day.  
- Morning sessions per computer = 3  
- Afternoon sessions per computer = 2  

**Step 2:**  
Add the morning and afternoon sessions for one computer.  
\(3 + 2 = 5\) sessions per computer per day.

**Step 3:**  
Multiply the sessions per computer by the number of computers.  
\(12 \text{ computers} \times 5 \text{ sessions/computer} = 60\) sessions.

**Final Answer:** 60

QUESTION 3: Neha submits before Omar. Omar submits before Priya. Karan submits after Priya. Who submits first and last?

--- DIRECT PROMPT ---
 Neha submits first, and Karan submits last.

--- CHAIN-OF-THOUGHT ---
 **Step 1:**  
List the given relationships.  
- Neha submits before Omar → Neha < Omar  
- Omar submits before Priya → Omar < Priya  
- Karan submits after Priya → Karan > Priya  

**Step 2:**  
Chain the inequalities.  
From Neha < Omar and Omar < Priya we get  
Neha < Omar < Priya.  

**Step 3:**  
Insert Karan using the third relation.  
Since Karan > Priya, Karan comes after Priya.  
Thus the full order is  
Neha < Omar < Priya < Karan.  

**Step 4:**  
Identify the first and last submitters.  
- First (earliest) submitter: **Neha**  
- Last (latest) submitter: **Karan**  

Final Answer: Neha first, Karan last.
